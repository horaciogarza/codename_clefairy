**Description:**

Welcome to Memorandum, the ultimate test of your memory and reflexes! Watch the emojis, memorize the sequence, and tap them back in order. It starts simple, but how long can you last as the patterns get longer, faster, and trickier?

Perfect for a quick mental workout or a long session of focus, Memorandum offers two unique ways to play:

*   **Classic Mode:** Race against the clock in a thrilling challenge. Your speed and accuracy are key. Can you beat the timer and conquer the boss battles that appear every five levels?

*   **Zen Mode:** Prefer to play at your own pace? Relax and sharpen your mind without the pressure of a timer. It's all about focus and flow.

Build up your heat with quick, correct taps to unleash FRENZY MODE and double your points! Climb the leaderboards, beat your own high score, and become a true Memorandum master.

Featuring a vibrant and playful design, engaging sound effects, and simple, intuitive gameplay, Memorandum is an addictive puzzle game for all ages. Download now and put your memory to the test!
